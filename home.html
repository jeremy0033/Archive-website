DIGITAL HERITAGE ARCHIVE

Preserving the history of the Hokonui Fashion Awards.

The Hokonui Archive is a digital collection preserving garments, runway photography and designer stories from the Hokonui Fashion Awards. Each year a new collection is added, allowing the archive to grow while documenting Southland's fashion history.

The archive currently features the 2025 collection.
